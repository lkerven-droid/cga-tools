CGA Math Curriculum Map

Curiosity Grove Academy  ·  Two-band model  ·  Based on CCSS progressions

K–2 Band

Concrete, hands-on, playful. The environment and materials do most of the teaching. Kids are building number sense, learning to count and compose, and beginning to add and subtract with meaning. Fractions aren't formal yet — just sharing and partitioning language. Units run shorter because attention spans are shorter and concepts need lots of repetition across different contexts.

3–6 Band

Conceptual depth over coverage. Multiplication and division are the gateway to everything — fractions, decimals, ratios, expressions. Units are longer because kids need time to encounter each concept in multiple ways: concrete, visual, abstract, and applied. The "go deep" units are the ones worth slowing down for, even if it means other things get less time.
